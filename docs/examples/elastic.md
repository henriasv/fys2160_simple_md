# Elastic constants, Debye and Einstein

A Lennard-Jones crystal is stretched and sheared by a small strain at low
temperature; the stress gives its elastic constants C11, C12 and C44, and these a
speed of sound and a Debye temperature. The atoms' own vibrations, from their
velocities, are compared with the Debye and Einstein models, and so is the heat
capacity they imply.
