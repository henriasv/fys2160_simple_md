# Quartz, sound and heat capacity

Alpha-quartz with the BKS force field: relaxed at 300 K and zero pressure with an
anisotropic barostat, stretched and sheared by 1% to measure its six elastic
constants against resonant-ultrasound measurements, and turned into speeds of sound
and a Debye temperature. The vibrational spectrum of the atoms shows the optical
bands the Debye model leaves out, and why quartz has only about 60% of its classical
heat capacity at room temperature.
